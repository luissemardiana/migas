# Home refinery background: close industrial view

Source: [Aerial View of Oil Refinery Plant](https://www.pexels.com/video/aerial-view-of-oil-refinery-plant-10396407/), Tom Fisk, Pexels.

License: [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/), checked 5 November 2026. The footage is used as an adapted decorative website background, not as a claim about the training provider's facilities.

The user requested a more visibly recognizable oil and gas refinery. This shot shows the process towers, pipe racks, steel structures and operating plant at night, replacing the distant waterfront view.

Processing: first five seconds of 3840 × 2160 footage, half speed, 1920 × 1080 at 25 fps. The final two seconds blend into the first two seconds for an eight-second repeating loop. Five-frame weighted temporal smoothing is applied across the loop boundary. No CSS zoom or end-of-video switch is used.

- `refinery-plant-hd.webp`: desktop animation, 1600 × 900 at 20 fps.
- `refinery-plant-mobile.webp`: 540 × 720 at 20 fps, cropped around the process towers.
- `refinery-plant.gif`: 1280 × 720 animated GIF fallback.
- `refinery-plant-poster.jpg` (1920 × 1080) and `refinery-plant-mobile-poster.jpg` (720 × 960): matching first frames for loading, unavailable animation, and reduced-motion preferences.

Animations repeat indefinitely. Modern browsers receive the smaller animated WebP instead of the GIF; all media is served locally.
